# Professional Dashboard Build Guide

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
Canvas: 16:9.
Header: “Executive KPI Command Center”.
Cards: Net Revenue, Gross Revenue, Orders, Active Customers, AOV, Repeat Rate.
Charts:
1. Monthly Revenue — line.
2. Monthly Growth — columns.
3. Top 10 Products — horizontal bar.
4. Revenue by Country — horizontal bar.
Slicers: Date, Country, Product.

## Page 2 — Customer Engagement
KPIs: Active Customers, Repeat Customer Rate, Orders/Customer, Revenue/Customer.
Charts: monthly customers, customer order-frequency distribution, country/customer table.

## Page 3 — Product Performance
KPIs: Units Sold, AOV, Revenue Contribution.
Charts: Top products, units vs revenue scatter, monthly product trend.

## Page 4 — Data Quality
Show raw row count, duplicate count, missing CustomerID, cancellation rows, negative quantity rows, zero/negative price rows.

## Design system
- Background: #F6F8FB
- Card: #FFFFFF
- Primary: #0F172A
- Accent: #2563EB
- Positive: #16A34A
- Warning: #F59E0B
- Negative: #DC2626
- Font: Segoe UI / Aptos
- Use one primary accent and semantic colors only.
- Avoid 3D charts, excessive borders and rainbow palettes.
